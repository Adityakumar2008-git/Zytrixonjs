Section 01

Executive Overview & Operating Entity

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Zytrixon ("Zytrixon", "we", "our", or "us"), operating as a pure business strategy, executive management consulting, performance marketing, and campaign architecture firm, collects, utilizes, maintains, and safeguards information gathered from visitors, prospective enterprise partners, and retained clients across our digital properties, including zytrixon.com and affiliated engagement portals.

Unlike conventional digital agencies or programmatic ad brokers, Zytrixon operates as an embedded strategic growth partner to C-suite leadership, enterprise founders, and political organizations. We recognize that our advisory engagements necessitate continuous access to sensitive financial metrics, customer acquisition economics, organizational hierarchies, and competitive roadmaps. Protecting this data is integral to our fiduciary commitment and institutional reputation.

Section 02

Information We Collect

In the course of delivering diagnostics, advisory proposals, and retainer operations, Zytrixon collects the following categories of information:

  • Executive Identification Coordinates: Full name, professional role or title, corporate enterprise name, business email address, direct telephone number, and communication preferences.
  • Commercial & Strategic Metrics: Current annualized run-rate, monthly revenue brackets, pricing tier structures, target growth milestones, customer acquisition costs (CAC), and identified operational bottlenecks provided during intake.
  • Consulting Engagement Details: Selected engagement models (e.g., Diagnostic Sprint, Advisory Retainer, Growth Partner Retainer), project scopes, deliverables milestones, and confidential briefing decks.
  • Technical Telemetry & Website Analytics: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referral URLs, session durations, and user interaction signals collected via anonymized first-party telemetry to ensure site performance and smooth navigation.
🛡️ Strict Policy on Consumer Personal Data

Zytrixon does not engage in consumer surveillance, cross-site identity harvesting, or the brokerages of third-party consumer personal records. We only process business-to-business (B2B) information necessary for strategic consulting and campaign execution.

Section 03

Purpose & Strategic Use of Information

Information submitted to Zytrixon is utilized strictly for lawful commercial and operational purposes, specifically:

  • Diagnostic Analysis: Reviewing company stages, unit economics, and pipeline friction to formulate actionable preliminary advisory roadmaps.
  • Advisory Engagement Delivery: Executing retainer deliverables across business strategy, sales pipeline restructuring, social media brand moats, performance acquisition funnels, and electoral campaign war rooms.
  • Direct Executive Communications: Responding to consultation requests, scheduling confidential diagnostic calls, and dispatching engagement invoices.
  • Executive Dispatch: Transmitting fortnightly strategic insights, unit economics teardowns, and proprietary consulting frameworks to opted-in subscribers. You may opt out of dispatch communications at any time.
Section 04

Non-Disclosure & Trade Secrets Protection

Zytrixon treats all non-public commercial figures, cap table information, margin calculations, internal ad account metrics, and campaign war room intelligence as Strictly Confidential Trade Secrets.

🔒 Our Binding Non-Disclosure Commitment

We enforce comprehensive internal Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) across all senior partners, fractional advisors, and tactical specialists. Client data is never shared with competitors, never published without explicit written executive consent, and never aggregated into public benchmark studies without complete irreversible anonymization.

Case studies showcased on our digital properties represent verified transformations where client identities have either been explicitly authorized for publication or strictly pseudonymized to preserve strategic confidentiality.

Section 05

Cloud Infrastructure & Webhook Security

To maintain instantaneous advisory response times and seamless database synchronizations, our intake infrastructure operates on high-security enterprise cloud environments:

  • Encrypted Transport: All form transmissions across /contact.html, /onboarding.html, and /insights.html utilize TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption protocols to prevent transit interception.
  • Enterprise Cloud Webhook: Form payloads are transmitted via private Google Cloud Apps Script endpoints directly into secure, restricted-access Google Cloud Workspace databases. Access is limited strictly to authorized Zytrixon senior partners via multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Zero Data Reselling: Zytrixon does not sell, rent, monetize, or barter client or prospect data under any circumstance.
Section 06

Marketing Platforms & Campaign Data Governance

For clients retaining Zytrixon for Performance Marketing, Social Media Authority, or Political Campaign Design:

  • Client-Owned Advertising Accounts: We mandate that all paid media budgets and ad accounts (Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager) remain under the direct ownership and billing control of the client enterprise. Zytrixon accesses ad networks strictly as an authorized administrative partner.
  • Audience Targeting Compliance: All audience segmentation, micro-targeting, and electorate communication models designed by Zytrixon comply strictly with prevailing advertising policies, data protection regulations, and electoral conduct rules in the governing jurisdiction.
  • First-Party Data Hygiene: Client customer lists or CRM exports uploaded for custom audience matching are transmitted via hashed protocols and purged upon campaign conclusion.
Section 07

Data Retention & Erasure Protocols

Zytrixon retains commercial intelligence only for the duration necessary to satisfy strategic advisory objectives, fulfill contractual obligations, comply with statutory tax and accounting standards, or resolve formal legal disputes.

Upon formal termination of an advisory engagement or upon written request from an authorized corporate officer, Zytrixon will initiate data erasure protocols to purge internal working spreadsheets, raw CRM extracts, and proprietary internal files within thirty (30) business days, retaining only statutory accounting and billing records required by law.

Section 08

Cookies & Digital Telemetry Policy

Our website utilizes minimal, essential first-party cookies and session storage mechanisms necessary to enable smooth architectural animations (Lenis smooth scrolling, GSAP state management), maintain user interface preferences, and prevent automated spam submissions.

We do not utilize aggressive cross-site retargeting cookies or third-party behavioral fingerprinting trackers on our informational pages. You may configure your browser settings to reject cookies; however, certain visual enhancements and interactive transitions may experience reduced functionality.

Section 09

Statutory Compliance & Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is formulated in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.

Any disputes, claims, or controversies arising out of or relating to this Privacy Policy or the processing of data by Zytrixon shall be governed exclusively by the laws of India, with exclusive jurisdiction resting in the competent courts located in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India.
