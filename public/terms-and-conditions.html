Section 02

The Five Strategic Advisory Disciplines

Zytrixon delivers specialized advisory across five distinct disciplines, each subject to defined operating frameworks:

  • 1. Business Strategy: Forensic financial audits, pricing tier recalibrations, unit economics modeling, cap table optimization, runway analysis, and competitive moat engineering.
  • 2. Business Consulting: C-suite executive war rooms, B2B sales pipeline acceleration, fractional Chief Growth Officer (CGO) deployment, organizational velocity audits, and post-merger integration.
  • 3. Social Media Management & Authority: Founder thought leadership architecture, viral organic content systems, algorithmic distribution models, and institutional narrative defensibility.
  • 4. Performance Marketing & Digital Acquisition: Full-funnel paid media architecture (Meta, Google, LinkedIn), high-ROAS cohort retargeting, conversion rate optimization (CRO), and blended CAC compression.
  • 5. Political Campaign Design: Micro-targeted constituency polling, data-driven demographic sentiment analytics, digital narrative war rooms, and ground-level voter mobilization architecture.

Section 04

Client Obligations & Financial Ground Truth

The compounding efficacy of Zytrixon advisory is contingent upon total executive transparency. Clients agree to:

  • Provide Truthful Data: Furnish authentic, unvarnished corporate financials, historical cohort data, margin breakdowns, customer churn statistics, and ad account telemetry.
  • Stakeholder Accessibility: Ensure designated C-level executives, founders, or campaign directors attend scheduled strategic war rooms and review deliverables within agreed turnaround intervals.
  • Operational Authority: Empower the internal implementation teams to deploy agreed-upon structural changes across marketing funnels, sales playbooks, or organizational workflows.
Section 05

Performance Marketing & Media Spend Disclaimers

For engagements involving performance acquisition, media buying strategy, or paid digital advertising:

📊 Paid Media Capital & Platform Rules

All advertising media budgets (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter/X) are funded directly by the Client via Client-owned payment methods attached to Client-owned ad accounts. Zytrixon advisory fees represent strategic management, architecture, and optimization services only; they do not include media spend capital.

Market Variance Acknowledgment: While Zytrixon applies rigorous mathematical modeling and proven acquisition frameworks (achieving historical results such as +180% margin and 4.2x revenue scale), the Client expressly acknowledges that digital ad auction dynamics, algorithmic shifts by third-party platforms (Meta, Google), seasonal consumer behavior, and macroeconomic shifts are beyond Zytrixon's direct control. Zytrixon does not issue speculative guarantees of specific monetary ROI.

Section 06

Political Campaign Advisory & Operational Code

Zytrixon accepts select political campaign design and voter mobilization mandates subject to stringent operational protocols:

  • Statutory & Electoral Compliance: All voter communication strategies, digital broadcasts, and field mobilization frameworks adhere strictly to the Model Code of Conduct, Representation of the People Act, 1951, and directives issued by the Election Commission of India (ECI).
  • Data Integrity: Voter sentiment research, constituency telemetry, and booth-level analytics are conducted strictly within public domain regulations and authorized survey frameworks.

Section 07

Intellectual Property Rights Allocation

To maintain crystal-clear commercial boundaries, intellectual property is demarcated as follows:

  • Client Intellectual Property: Upon full and final settlement of all applicable retainer fees, the Client retains exclusive ownership of all bespoke deliverables created specifically for the Client, including tailored strategic decks, brand guideline books, custom ad creatives, and proprietary organizational roadmaps.
  • Zytrixon Proprietary Pre-Existing IP: Zytrixon retains sole and exclusive ownership of all general methodologies, diagnostic algorithms, financial calculation models, benchmarking frameworks, proprietary slide templates, and unreleased strategic playbooks developed prior to or independently of the engagement. The Client receives a perpetual, non-transferable internal license to utilize such frameworks within their ongoing operations.
Section 08

Mutual Non-Disclosure & Fiduciary Duty

Both parties agree that during the term of engagement and for a period of three (3) years thereafter, neither party shall disclose to any third party any confidential, non-public technical, commercial, financial, or strategic information obtained during the partnership without prior written consent.

Zytrixon is authorized to reference the Client's enterprise name and high-level metric transformation (e.g., "1.5x to 4.2x Revenue in 8 Months") in verified case study archives, provided all sensitive underlying unit figures and customer identities remain protected.

Section 09

Fees, Invoicing, Taxes & Retainer Renewals

Financial administration of all Zytrixon advisory engagements is governed by the following commercial terms:

  • Advance Invoicing: Monthly retainers are invoiced thirty (30) days in advance of the service period. Invoices are payable within seven (7) business days of issuance (Net-7).
  • Applicable Statutory Taxes: All stated consulting fees are exclusive of statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST @ 18%), which will be added to formal invoices in accordance with Indian tax statutes.
  • Suspension for Non-Payment: If an invoice remains unpaid past fourteen (14) days from issuance, Zytrixon reserves the right to suspend partner advisory sessions, pause campaign war room supervision, and reassign partner bandwidth.
  • Non-Refundable Retainers: Because retainer agreements reserve dedicated, non-recoverable senior partner bandwidth and institutional resources, retainer installments paid are non-refundable once the billing month commences.

Advisory Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability

⚖️ Commercial Advisory Nature Disclaimer

Zytrixon acts strictly in an advisory and consulting capacity. Strategic frameworks, diagnostic recommendations, pipeline forecasts, and marketing architectures represent professional opinions based on available enterprise data. The ultimate authority and legal responsibility for making commercial, investment, operational, product, or political campaign decisions rests solely with the Client's authorized corporate officers and board of directors.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, in no event shall Zytrixon, its partners, advisors, directors, or tactical specialists be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, consequential, or special damages (including loss of prospective business profits, loss of goodwill, downtime, or commercial disruptions) arising out of or in connection with the advisory services.

Zytrixon's total aggregate liability arising out of any engagement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall be strictly capped at the total professional fees actually paid by the Client to Zytrixon in the three (3) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

Section 11

Term, Renewal & Off-Boarding Procedures

Unless otherwise stipulated in an executed Statement of Work (SOW):

  • Initial Commitment Horizon: Advisory Retainer engagements carry a minimum initial term of ninety (90) days; Growth Partner mandates carry an initial term of one hundred eighty (180) days.
  • Orderly Termination Notice: Following the initial commitment horizon, either party may terminate the ongoing monthly retainer by providing thirty (30) days prior written notice via corporate email.
  • Orderly Off-Boarding: During the 30-day notice period, Zytrixon will ensure an orderly transition of all live campaign accounts, hand over finalized strategic documentation, and archive proprietary working files.
Section 12

Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms and any contractual engagements executed hereunder shall be construed, interpreted, and governed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India, without regard to conflict of law principles.

In the event of any controversy or dispute, the parties agree to first attempt informal executive resolution in good faith for twenty-one (21) days. If unresolved, the dispute shall be referred to binding arbitration conducted under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with a sole arbitrator mutually appointed by the parties. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India, and the proceedings shall be conducted in English.
